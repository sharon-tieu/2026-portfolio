About me

I'm Sharon Tieu. I started out writing documentation, which meant years of watching the gap between how software is built and how people try to use it. Design was the obvious next step: fix the screen so the manual gets shorter.

I'm Vietnamese-American and moved to Manhattan for my role at JPMorgan Chase. Outside work I'm usually out with friends or finding a new spot somewhere in the city.
